Australian household energy

What your appliances actually cost to run.

Appliances are the part of a power bill households have the most direct control over, yet the running cost of a device is rarely visible at the point of purchase. This site brings power ratings, typical usage patterns and electricity prices together so the yearly cost of an appliance is easy to see before you buy it.
