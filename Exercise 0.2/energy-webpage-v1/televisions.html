Televisions

Screen size and panel technology drive most of the difference in television power use. A larger panel needs more light output, and brightness is where most of the energy goes. Reading the numbers

Rated power is a ceiling

Placeholder text. A television rarely draws its full rated power. Bright scenes and high brightness settings push it closer to the maximum.
